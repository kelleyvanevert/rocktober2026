

ARCHITECTURE

 app thread                                         audio thread (cpal callback)
 ───────────                                        ──────────────────────────────
 text ─▶ parse ─▶ eval ─▶ Sound (description)
                           │ play(): instantiate
                           ▼
                   Box<dyn Node> ── commands queue ─▶ Engine: pop commands,
                   (built, allocated, samples         mix active voices,
                    already decoded)                   remove finished ones
                                                          │
            drop it here ◀── garbage queue ───────────────┘


    - audio thread should never block (no files, allocations, mutexes, printing)
    - `rtrb` = lock-free queue (like SC OSC messages)
        - audio nodes are sent back via queue to main thread for deallocation

        - (commands, commands_rx)
        - (garbage_tx, garbage)

    - nodes are pull-based
        - Sampler
        - Fit
        - Repeat
