-- Two-step: the backbone of most drum & bass. Set Ableton to 172-176 bpm.
-- Drum Rack notes: c1 kick, d1 snare, f#1 closed hat.

at x.0.0 { note c1 }              -- kick on 1
at x.2.2 { note c1 }              -- and on the "and" of 3
at x.1|3.0 { note d1 }            -- snare on 2 and 4
every 2 { note f#1 vel 50+r30 }   -- eighth-note hats, slightly uneven
